SEJONG205 is the operating organization behind SEJONGO, a multi-vendor platform dedicated to Korean and Korea-inspired products in Europe. We provide the data, infrastructure, and operational framework that help suppliers enter, sell, and scale in the European market.

What SEJONG205 Is

SEJONG205 operates as an Operational Support Organization, not as a traditional retailer. We build a stable and open environment where vendors define their own sales strategies while customers experience a trusted marketplace.

What We Solve

European customers search across multiple platforms to find Korean products, while suppliers struggle to reach broader audiences. SEJONG205 centralizes discovery and comparison into one integrated experience.

Through this structure, customers gain wider choice and confidence, while suppliers naturally increase their market exposure—creating a win-win ecosystem.

SEJONG205 does not manage or control vendors. Instead, we act as a strategic partner, supporting suppliers with environment, information, data, and operational tools to enable stable sales and long-term growth in Europe.
